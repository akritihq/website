A research meeting in topological data analysis

Sangam 2027

संगम saṅgama · Sanskrit, n. — a confluence: the place where rivers meet.

Monday 14 – Friday 18 June 2027 · IIT Mandi, Kamand, Himachal Pradesh

A meeting where topological data analysis and computational geometry are in the same room. Held immediately before SoCG / CG Week 2027 at IISc Bangalore — the first SoCG in India — so that a single trip covers both, and a school in the days beforehand feeds directly into it.

Programme

One room, one track.

A single-track meeting with no parallel sessions. The intent is a room that stays together — where a topologist hears the geometry talks and a computational geometer hears the statistics, rather than two audiences passing in a corridor.

Five days of a single track leaves room for a substantially larger programme than a short meeting would, and a good part of it will come from contributed submissions rather than invitations. Talks will be ordered by accessibility across the days — surveys and expository work early, technical work later — and every invited speaker will be asked for a short accessible preamble, since a good part of the room will have arrived from the school rather than from the field.

Evenings

Laptops, not posters

Flash-talk presenters sit with their laptops open, refreshments out, and people circulate. It keeps the one-to-one conversation a poster session gives you, without asking anyone to carry print tubes across the country.

Midweek

An afternoon out

A half-day excursion to Rewalsar — a pilgrimage lake sacred to Buddhists, Hindus and Sikhs — and the old temples of Mandi town, about 25 km away. Optional, and back by early evening. tbc

Afterwards

On to Bangalore

SoCG 2027 and CG Week run 21–25 June at IISc Bangalore, leaving the weekend to travel. A half-day CG Week workshop on topology and computational geometry is planned as a companion event. proposal pending

Beforehand

A school, in the days before.

A separate introductory school in topological data analysis is being planned at IIT Mandi for the days immediately preceding Sangam, organised locally and funded independently of this meeting. It is intended for students and newcomers to the field, and its participants are welcome to register for Sangam and stay on. dates, format and funding tbc

The two events are deliberately separate — different organisers, different budgets, different audiences — but scheduled so that a student can arrive knowing no persistent homology and leave having heard current research, and then continue to CG Week if they wish. Details will be published by the school's organisers.

Where

Kamand, Himachal Pradesh.

The venue is the Kamand campus of the Indian Institute of Technology Mandi, in the Himalayan foothills about 14 km above Mandi town at around a thousand metres — roughly 15–25 °C in June. Participants are housed on campus, with the mess on site.

Getting there

Via Chandigarh

Coaches from Chandigarh to Kamand at the start of the meeting, and back at the end. Detailed travel guidance, visa support letters and group booking will follow once the meeting is confirmed. tbc

In June

Cool, and green

The valley sits high enough to escape the plains heat. Expect mild days, cool evenings, and the first hints of the monsoon toward the end of the month.

Nearby

Mandi town

Known as the Varanasi of the hills for its density of old temples, and the natural stop for anyone arriving early or staying on.
