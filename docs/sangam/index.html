A school and research meeting in topological data analysis

Sangam2027

संगम saṅgama · Sanskrit, n. — a confluence: the place where rivers meet.

14 – 18 June 2027 · IIT Mandi, Kamand, Himachal Pradesh venue tbc

Five days: mornings are a hands-on school, afternoons are a research meeting, and everyone is in the same room for both. Held the week before SoCG / CG Week 2027 at IISc Bangalore — the first SoCG in India — so that students can continue on to the conference and lecturers can combine the two trips.

Proposed · nothing yet confirmed ~40 students Satellite of SoCG 2027
Planning stage

This event is proposed, not scheduled. The host agreement, the venue, the funding and every invitation are still to come. Dates, programme and committee below are a working plan and will change.

Items still to be settled are marked tbc throughout. Applications are expected to open in January 2027; there is nothing to apply to yet. If you would like to hear when there is, write to hello@akriti.io.

At a glance

One week, two halves, no parallel sessions.

DatesMon 14 – Fri 18 June 2027
VenueIIT Mandi, Kamand tbc
Cohort~40 students tbc
ApplicationsOpen Jan 2027

The school and the research meeting are interleaved rather than run back to back. Students attend both halves; speakers have every morning free and miss no talk. There are no parallel sessions — a programme where the lecturers are in the room for the research talks is what makes the rest of the design work.

Mornings · school
One lecturer, one chapter
A single lecturer owns the whole morning, alternating teaching with hands-on work rather than lecturing first and running a lab afterwards. Five mornings, five topics, ~8 hours of teaching against ~12 hours at the keyboard.
Afternoons · research meeting
Talks, then a digest
Four talk slots, a block of five-minute flash talks, and then a talk digest — thirty minutes in which one of the school lecturers unpacks the afternoon's talks in the vocabulary the students have by that point.
Evenings · discussion
Laptops, not posters
Flash-talk presenters sit with their laptops open, refreshments out, and people circulate. It keeps the one-to-one conversation a poster session gives you, without asking students to carry print tubes across the country.

Labs run in two parallel streams by background — same material, different pace. The admitted cohort will span motivated undergraduates to PhD students, and a single stream would be simultaneously too slow for a third of the room and too fast for another third. Wednesday afternoon is free: a half-day excursion to Rewalsar and Mandi town, with the lab room left open for anyone catching up. tbc

Software

The labs run on Akriti.

Akriti is an open ecosystem for topological data analysis in Python: one persistence-diagram format that every backend can speak, the statistical layer Python has been missing, and a maintained home for published TDA methods whoever wrote them. Computation is delegated to GUDHI and Ripser rather than reimplemented. Apache-2.0.

Using it across all five mornings means a student's Tuesday notebook still runs on Friday, and the code they write in the school is the code they take home. Everything is published afterwards, so the material outlives the week.

Before you arrive
Nothing to install
A preconfigured environment ships in advance and travels on USB sticks as well, because forty people cannot all reach the internet at once. Two online primer sessions in May 2027 cover the linear algebra, the topology vocabulary and the Python setup, so that contact hours go on TDA. tbc
During the week
Teaching notebooks
Worked, annotated and self-explanatory — not exercise sheets that assume you caught everything in the lecture. Each lecturer's notebook is designed in lockstep with their teaching segments, as one artifact rather than slides handed to a TA.
Afterwards
Yours to reuse
The full pack is released openly for anyone to teach from, alongside the existing Akriti teaching pack. Adapt it, run it in your own course, tell us what confused your students.
If you maintain a TDA library, we would rather build on it than around it. The diagram interchange specification is public and open to comment, and a method with no Python implementation is welcome to have its home here — cited and attributed.

Speakers to be announced.

Sixteen talk slots across four afternoons — two keynotes, twelve invited talks, and two panel or demo sessions — plus around twenty-eight flash talks selected from the applications. No invitations have gone out. First-wave invitations are planned for autumn 2026, and names will appear here only once they have been accepted.

Talks are ordered by accessibility across the week — surveys and expository talks early, technical talks on Thursday and Friday, by which point students have had eight teaching segments behind them. Some talks will still go over some heads. That is deliberate: hearing what the frontier sounds like is part of what a week like this is for.

Where

Kamand, Himachal Pradesh.

Everyone is housed on campus with the mess on site, which is the only reason an 08:00 start is reasonable. hosting request not yet made; hostel capacity unverified

Afterwards

An edited volume, in two parts.

The school produces a book, not just a week. It is planned for Applied Geometry & Topology (Chapman & Hall/CRC), in two parts: five chapters of lecture notes, one per school day, and a set of refereed research contributions from speakers who wish to contribute. tbc

Deciding this at the outset changes how the school is prepared — lecturers write chapter-quality notes rather than slides — and it is the part of the week that is still useful to the next cohort. It is an edited volume with an open invitation to contribute, not proceedings: nobody should expect to find every talk in it.
